About Our Cafe

Serving happiness in every cup since 2026

Our Story

Started in the heart of Sector 11, Chandigarh, Brew & Bean Cafe was created with a simple mission: to offer college students and coffee lovers an affordable, peaceful place to relax, study, and connect.

We source 100% Arabica coffee beans directly from estates in Coorg and Chikmagalur. Whether you need a strong Espresso before your morning lecture or a comforting Masala Chai in the evening, we prepare every order fresh.
